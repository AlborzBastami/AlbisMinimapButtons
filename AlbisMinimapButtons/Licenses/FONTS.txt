Modifications: PT Sans Narrow, Lato and Fira Sans are the unmodified original
files. All other files are static instances (Regular, or Bold for Roboto Bold)
generated with fontTools from the variable fonts published by Google Fonts.
None of these fonts declare a Reserved Font Name that applies to the modified
files. The fonts are not sold separately; they are bundled with this addon.
